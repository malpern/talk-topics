The collaborators

The project started with my nephews.

They were interested in one wonderfully specific comparison: the value of sports cards versus Pokémon cards. That curiosity became the entire product brief.

The question

Sports legend or Pokémon legend—which card is worth more?

Simple enough for a five-year-old to explain. Surprising enough to carry twenty rounds.

The game loop

One round. Three beats.

01 · face offShow one sports card and one Pokémon card.The names are visible. The prices are not.
02 · make a pickChoose the card that feels more valuable.No spreadsheet. No auction history. Just a guess.
03 · make it loudReveal both prices and celebrate—or groan—together.Then move automatically toward the #1 finale.

Designed for playing together

The reaction is part of the interface.

The point is not to study a price list. It is to pick a side, discover the answer together, and immediately want the next matchup.

Prototype screen · the complete card image/value pairing audit is still pending

What the room demanded

Design for nephews, not for the repository.

01Fit the whole game on one screen—no scrolling during a round.
02Hide every price until someone commits to a card.
03Make correct and incorrect answers unmistakable.
04Keep the countdown moving before the energy disappears.
