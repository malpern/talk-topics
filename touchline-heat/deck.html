The audio engine

ElevenLabs turned the brief into a soundtrack.

BriefChoose two soccer archetypes and the sound of their showdown.
PlanStructure lyrics, alternating verses, choruses, and musical style.
APISend the song plan to ElevenLabs Music and receive a full MP3 battle.
StageSynchronize the track with highlights, lyrics, player emphasis, and voting.

Original parody songs · not real quotes or voice clones

What made it work

Pictures were not enough. Short voice lines were not enough. The product had to feel like a real song over a real match.

The bar kept moving toward the thing my nephew could describe immediately: watch the soccer+hear the battle.

Takeaway

A seven-year-old did not ask for an AI demo. He asked for soccer stars to sing their own showdown.

The technology disappeared into the loop: choose a clash → hear the song → watch the highlights → settle it with a vote.
