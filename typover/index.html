Why Typover

The best correction is the one that doesn’t break the thought.

The product promise
“Fix the obvious. Mark the change. Give it back.”

Typover acts only when it can verify the smallest exact range. Ambiguous text stays untouched. Apple’s on-device intelligence remains the default.
