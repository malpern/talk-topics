How WebMCP coaching works ↗

Same maze. A different setting.

This browser preview supports solo, local two-human play, and a local image-only partner. Switching tabs pauses the game. Online co-op and Equalized timing remain in the native app; browser trials do not qualify network fairness.

Heuristics can make mistakes. A high score in one short trial is not proof of a better policy. Ghosts, sprites and sound use the game’s documented ROM-derived assets.
